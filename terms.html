The App

TimeCube helps you focus and build habits. It includes:

  • A focus timer that rewards sessions of 15 minutes or more with a card draw
  • A collection of nearly 300 cards across 9 series and 6 rarities
  • Tasks, routines, daily and weekly habits, and optional calendar sync
  • Points, a Shop, a Time Bank and custom rewards
  • Live Activities, widgets and an Apple Watch app
