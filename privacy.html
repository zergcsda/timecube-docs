Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. TimeCube does not run its own servers for your data, does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

To make the app work, TimeCube stores the following on your device:

  • Focus sessions, card draws and your card collection
  • Tasks, routines, habits and check-ins
  • Points, Shop history, Time Bank balance and custom rewards
  • Settings, timer skins and your parental PIN

This information is used only to provide TimeCube’s features. We cannot see it.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, TimeCube syncs this data through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) so it stays the same on your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It is stored by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy and protected by your Apple Account. We have no access to it.

Device features and permissions

  • Calendar (optional): with your permission, TimeCube reads your calendar events to show them alongside your tasks and can add task events to your calendar. Calendar data stays in your calendar and on your devices.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Live Activities and widgets: show your focus progress on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and on the Home Screen, using data on your device.
  • Apple Watch: your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange TimeCube data directly with each other.
  • Motion: used only to give cards a light-shimmer effect as you tilt your device. Motion readings are not stored.

Purchases

TimeCube Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes all payments; we never receive your payment details. Earlier monthly and yearly subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings.

Children

TimeCube is designed to be suitable for the whole family. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not collect personal information from children. The app has no ads, and a parental PIN can protect sensitive settings.

Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar laws)

Because your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, you are always in control of it:

  • Access and edit: view and change your data in the app.
  • Delete: delete data in the app, delete the app, or remove TimeCube’s iCloud data in your device’s iCloud settings.
  • No selling or sharing: we do not sell or share personal information, including for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them on request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
