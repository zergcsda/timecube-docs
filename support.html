Common questions

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Sign in with the Apple Account you bought it with, then tap Restore Purchases on the Pro page in the app.

I subscribed to Pro before. Do I still have it?

Yes. Earlier monthly and yearly subscriptions keep Pro unlocked while they are active. Pro is now a one-time purchase, and you can manage or cancel an old subscription anytime in your Apple Account subscription settings.

How does my data sync between iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch?

Through your iCloud. Make sure each device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on.

What are the odds for each rarity?

Every rarity’s odds are listed in the app. From 25 minutes of focus, Super Rare and above are 1.5× as likely; from 45 minutes, 2×.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
