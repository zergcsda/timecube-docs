Ran into a problem with TimeCube, or have an idea to make it better? Email us — every message is read by the people who build the app.

Email Support

To help us fix it fast, please include:

  1. The app version (you can find it on the App Store page or in Settings)
  2. Your device model and iOS / iPadOS version
  3. What happened, and what you expected to happen
  4. Steps to reproduce the problem, if you can
  5. Screenshots or a short screen recording, if possible

We reply to every email as soon as we can.
